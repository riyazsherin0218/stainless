Our Story

A Trading Business Built On Repeat Trust.

THE STAINLESS (INDIA) operates out of Madurai, Tamil Nadu, trading in stainless steel and metal scrap. The business sources material from sellers across the region, checks it, and moves it on to buyers who need consistent, well-sorted metal stock.

There's no large showroom or complicated process here — enquiries are handled directly, quotes are given plainly, and the same care goes into a small consignment as a large one.
