Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

THE STAINLESS (INDIA) ("we", "us", "our") operates this website to share information about our stainless steel and metal scrap trading business and to receive enquiries from prospective buyers and sellers.

Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry form on this website, we collect the information you choose to provide — such as your name, company name, phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, location, material details and any message or files you attach. This information is sent directly to us via WhatsApp or email; it is not stored on a server or database operated by this website.

How We Use It

We use the information you submit only to respond to your enquiry, provide quotes, and arrange the purchase, sale or supply of material. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Any technical browser storage used is limited to remembering your on-page preferences within a single visit.
