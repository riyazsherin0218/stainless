About Us

Metal Is Our Material.
Trust Is Our Standard.

THE STAINLESS (INDIA) trades, sources and supplies stainless steel and metal scrap for industrial buyers — built on straightforward dealing and consistent material quality.

Registered as a Micro Enterprise under the Ministry of MSME and GST-compliant, we work directly with businesses and individuals across Tamil Nadu who need dependable procurement and supply of metal materials — no middlemen, no guesswork.
